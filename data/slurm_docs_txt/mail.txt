Mailing Lists
SchedMD maintains two Slurm mailing lists:
slurm-announce@schedmd.com is designated for communications about Slurm releases. Only SchedMD may post to this list. Low traffic. 
slurm-users@schedmd.com (formerly slurm-dev@schedmd.com) is for discussion by the Slurm community, and is typically used by Slurm system administrators for discussion and community-provided support. (Please see the SchedMD support page page for commercial support options.) Any person subscribed to this mailing list may post to it. High traffic. 
You can subscribe to either list, change your subscription options, or unsubscribe through the Mailman interface here.
An archive of the slurm-users list is online:
http://groups.google.com/group/slurm-users
